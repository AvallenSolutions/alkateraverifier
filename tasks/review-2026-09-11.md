## B. Improvements for accuracy, transparency and reliability

Ranked. Effort S/M/L.

1. Evidence-grounded extraction: `{ value, page, quote }` per field, quote verified against
   page text, shown under every check and finding. L.
2. Blind, injection-hardened evaluation: redact producer identity and self-assessment,
   structured `evidence[]` paths per finding, word-boundary citations, red-team fixture. M.
3. Explicit `not_computable` checks and three-state gates (`passed | failed | not_assessable`);
   minimum check count for the hard gate; tier caps when core clauses were not in scope. M.
4. Versioned rubric and audit trail per verification: engine version, prompt hashes, model
   ids, clause snapshot, tolerances, PDF SHA-256; "What we checked and why" on result, PDF
   and badge. M.
5. Verifier-owned data-quality measures: impact-weighted primary share from
   `topContributors x materials`; consistency checks; DQR scale normalisation. M.
6. Cheap deterministic checks: species `mass x GWP`, GWP values vs AR5/AR6, share sums,
   contributors vs stages, recycled-content plus EoL-credit double counting, zero-impact
   categories need justification. M.
7. Self-consistency: second evaluator pass (or challenger); disagreement resolves to the
   less favourable result or `insufficient_info`, and is surfaced. M.
8. Worker robustness: `after(runVerification)` in `/api/verify`, authenticated retry route,
   `processing_started_at` + stale reclaim + cron sweeper, SDK timeout/retries, streaming,
   `max_tokens` handling, Sentry with ids, every Supabase error checked. M.
9. Cost controls: usage logged per call, per-user monthly cap, Haiku pre-screen for
   "is this an LCA", page and character caps, real prompt caching with 1h TTL. S/M.
10. PDF handling: text plus document block together, garbage-text detection, page selection
    by headings for long reports, encrypted-PDF message. M/L.
11. Units and bases: `unit` and `perBasis` per block, one primary functional unit,
    conversions, reconcile within one basis. M.
12. Multiple impact categories: stage sums for water and land where printed; "climate only"
    is a §4.4 coverage gap unless the goal justifies it. M.
13. Deterministic allocation flags feeding §4.3.4. S.
14. Non-English reports: language detection, verbatim quotes, one fixture. S.
15. Score floors per band; unstated data quality scores zero. S.
16. Badge and report honesty: report date/version and PDF hash, standards and clauses
    evaluated, confidence, engine version, three-state critical review, honest heading for
    Not Certified. S.
17. Operability: DB-backed rate limits, CHECK constraints and unique indexes, health
    endpoint, required-in-production env validation, refund handling, retention policy and
    user-initiated delete, minimal admin listing of stuck/failed runs, badge and PDF caching. M.

## C. Test gaps

- The success criterion ("100% of planted errors caught, zero false passes") is not measured:
  the flawed fixtures never touch the engine. Needs a recorded-response harness with golden
  outputs per fixture, plus a gated live eval with thresholds.
- No tests for: empty checks, empty findings, standards with no clauses, `insufficient_info`
  on gate clauses, empty functional unit, subtotal rows, headline-excludes-biogenic,
  positive credit sign, gram-scale reports, label-only "Good", score floors, duplicate
  findings per clause, wrong-clause substring citations, `max_tokens` and refusal stops,
  vision fallback, scanned/encrypted/non-English/non-LCA inputs, storage failures, long
  product names, concurrent claims, stuck reclaim, report and badge data, seed invariants
  (every active standard has a clause), route validation and rate limiting.
