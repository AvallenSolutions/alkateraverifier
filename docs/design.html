Anatomy · band, statement, paper, band

Sticky ink band on top (wordmark, mark, mono tabs with a 3px accent underline). The statement: teal eyebrow, one sentence, figures right over mono labels. The paper: cream panels, hairlines, radius 6, at most one saturated block (usually none — the Verifier is ink-led). Sticky ink band at the bottom: the standing independence-and-scope strip, plus the one quick action. No desk-link into alkatera·OS.
